# Claims and Compliance

## Standing Advisory Boundary

PressureTest is educational diligence software. It is not legal, tax, accounting, lending, financial, or investment advice.

## Claims Rules

Do not make unapproved claims about:

- Franchise failure rates.
- Average franchise profitability.
- Likelihood of success or failure.
- Comparative performance of brands.
- Guaranteed savings.
- Guaranteed risk reduction.
- Legal, tax, accounting, lending, or investment outcomes.

## Required Approval

Howard approval is required before:

- Publishing marketing content.
- Sending marketing content.
- Using customer information publicly.
- Making sourced claims that could be reputationally or legally consequential.
- Referencing specific franchise brands in a critical or comparative way.

## Compliance Checklist for Marketing Tasks

- Audience is defined.
- Channel is defined.
- Claims are sourced or avoided.
- Advisory boundary is preserved.
- No prohibited financial, legal, or success claims.
- No unapproved customer information.
- Howard approval is received before publication.
