# Design Standards

## Product Feel

PressureTest should feel like a calm, serious franchise diligence workspace, not a generic questionnaire or hype-heavy marketing funnel.

## UI Principles

- Prioritize clarity over density.
- Keep screens focused on the next decision.
- Use progressive disclosure for heavy diligence content.
- Preserve readable mobile layouts.
- Avoid alarming language and decorative excess.
- Reuse existing local UI helpers before adding new patterns.

## Website Standards

- `website/` is the authoritative marketing source.
- Keep public copy aligned with `PressureTest: Franchise`.
- Do not market Startup or Acquisition as active workflows unless Howard approves a product scope change.
- Do not publish claims without source and compliance review.

## Streamlit Standards

- Keep the seven-step Franchise Beta flow understandable.
- Do not add new major feature surfaces during routine setup or docs work.
- Preserve existing authentication and persistence behavior unless a task explicitly approves changes.
