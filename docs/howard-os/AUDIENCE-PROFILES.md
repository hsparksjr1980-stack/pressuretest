# Audience Profiles

## Primary Audience

Prospective franchise buyers and operators who are evaluating a specific opportunity before signing, borrowing, leasing, or investing.

## Common Jobs

- Organize diligence.
- Clarify assumptions.
- Identify missing evidence.
- Prepare questions for franchisors, franchisees, lenders, CPAs, attorneys, spouses, partners, or investors.
- Slow down a decision that is gaining momentum before the evidence is strong enough.

## Likely Concerns

- Working capital pressure.
- Local rent, labor, buildout, and ramp assumptions.
- FDD information that may not translate to their market.
- Personal guarantee or lease exposure.
- Whether the opportunity fits their time, skills, and downside tolerance.

## Secondary Audiences

- Spouses and business partners.
- Lenders and advisors reviewing the buyer's assumptions.
- Franchise consultants or attorneys who want clearer diligence artifacts.
