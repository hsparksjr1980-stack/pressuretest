# Brand Voice

## Voice

PressureTest should sound:

- Cautious.
- Fair.
- Direct.
- Practical.
- Skeptical but not cynical.
- Calm, not alarmist.
- Clear, not academic.
- Helpful, not salesy.

## Preferred Language

Use language such as:

- Based on the information provided.
- This may indicate risk.
- This should be verified.
- This could materially affect the decision.
- Local economics should be validated before further commitment.

## Avoid

- Guaranteed outcomes.
- Profit promises.
- Failure-rate claims without approved sourcing.
- Telling users to buy, invest, sign, or walk away.
- Legal, tax, accounting, lending, or investment advice.
- Bashing specific franchise brands.
- Fear-based or hype-based copy.

## Product Nouns

Preferred current public product name: `PressureTest: Franchise`.
