# Marketing Strategy

## Current Marketing Source

The marketing website lives in `website/` and is built with Next.js.

## Current Positioning

PressureTest: Franchise helps prospective franchise operators pressure-test assumptions before they sign, borrow, lease, or invest.

## Weekly Marketing Cycle

Each weekly cycle may include one approved marketing task. The task must come from a GitHub issue with:

- `marketing` label.
- `ready` label.
- Audience.
- Objective.
- Deliverable.
- Channel.
- Claims and compliance requirements.
- Current weekly cycle.

## Allowed Routine Agent Decisions

Agents may decide:

- Draft structure.
- Headline variants consistent with brand voice.
- Minor wording refinements.
- Internal filenames.
- SEO organization that does not make new claims.
- Formatting and editorial polish.

## Howard Approval Required

Howard must approve before marketing material is published, sent, scheduled, or used externally.
